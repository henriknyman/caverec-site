Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Cave Recorder (caverec) runs locally on your machine. We operate no backend servers and do not collect, receive, store, process, or share any personal information or user data of any kind.

No Data Collection

We do not collect personal identifiers, account credentials, usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, cookies, or tracking information. Neither the Cave Recorder desktop application nor this website uses any analytics services, advertising trackers, or third-party data processors.

Local Operation & User Files

All audio recordings, project sessions, configuration files, and mixdowns created in Cave Recorder are processed and stored locally on your device. If you explicitly configure optional third-party storage or cloud integrations (such as exporting or synchronizing sessions and mixdowns to your own cloud drive), any credentials, tokens, and files remain strictly on your device and are transferred directly between your machine and your chosen service provider without passing through any servers operated by us.

Google Drive Integration & Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

When you optionally connect your Google Drive account inside Cave Recorder to synchronize recording sessions or export mixdowns, Cave Recorder requests Google Drive API access solely to read, write, and synchronize the project session folders and mixdown exports you explicitly create, link, or configure.

OAuth 2.0 tokens and credentials are stored exclusively on your local device (~/.config/caverec/ or local session configuration) and communicate directly between your device and Google's API servers (googleapis.com) over HTTPS. Cave Recorder operates no intermediary servers and never receives, stores, views, or transmits your Google account data or audio files.

Cave Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect or have access to any user data, no information is ever sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
