Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Cave Recorder (caverec) does not collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal information or user data of any kind.

No Data Collection

We do not collect personal identifiers, account credentials, usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, cookies, or tracking information. Neither the application nor this website uses any analytics services, advertising trackers, or third-party data processors.

Local Operation & User Files

All audio recordings, project sessions, configuration files, and mixdowns created in Cave Recorder are processed and stored locally on your device. If you explicitly configure optional third-party storage or cloud integrations (such as exporting or uploading mixdowns to your own cloud drive), any credentials, tokens, and files remain strictly on your device and are transferred directly between your machine and your chosen service provider without passing through any servers operated by us.

Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect or have access to any user data, no information is ever sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
