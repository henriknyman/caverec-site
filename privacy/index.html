Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Cave Recorder (caverec) and Cave Companion (iOS, iPadOS, and Android) run locally on your devices. We operate no backend servers and do not collect, receive, store, process, or share any personal information or user data of any kind.

No Data Collection

We do not collect personal identifiers, account credentials, usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, cookies, or tracking information. Neither the Cave Recorder desktop application, the Cave Companion mobile application, nor this website uses any analytics services, advertising trackers, or third-party data processors.

Local Operation & User Files

All audio recordings, video captures, Projects, Sessions, Takes, Passes, live Prints, Mixdowns, and configuration files created in Cave Recorder and Cave Companion are processed and stored locally on your devices. In the Mac App Store edition, access to custom Session Library folders outside the app container is granted strictly through standard macOS file pickers and stored locally by macOS as security-scoped bookmarks. Local command-line control (caverec-cli) communicates strictly over owner-only local inter-process channels on the same computer. If you explicitly configure optional cloud synchronization to your own Google Drive, all credentials, tokens, and media files remain strictly on your devices and are transferred directly between your devices and Google's API servers without passing through any servers operated by us.

Cave Companion (iOS, iPadOS & Android) Permissions

The Cave Companion mobile application connects directly over your local Wi-Fi network to Cave Recorder on your studio computer (or runs completely offline in its bundled Sample Studio Session) and collects no personal data, analytics, or crash telemetry:

  • Camera: Used only when you scan a studio pairing QR code, record synchronized local studio video angles, or run optical flash sync calibration. Video files remain on your device and your paired local studio computer (or your own optional Google Drive).
  • Local Network (Wi-Fi): Used solely for local Bonjour (_caverec._udp) and LAN discovery, pairing, and exchanging live transport, console, stagebox monitor, metering, audio, and video streams directly with Cave Recorder on your local network without external relay servers.
  • On-Device Credential Storage: Studio pairing keys and optional Google OAuth tokens are stored exclusively in your device's operating-system secure credential storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore) and are never transmitted to us.

Google Drive Integration & Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

When you optionally connect your Google Drive account inside Cave Recorder or Cave Companion, the local application requests Google Drive API access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file or, when configured for cross-account linked remote project folders, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) solely to read, write, and synchronize the Projects, Sessions, Takes, Passes, live Prints, and Mixdowns you explicitly create, link, or configure, and reads your Google account email address solely to display the connected account badge in your local interface.

OAuth 2.0 tokens and credentials are stored exclusively on your local computer (~/Library/Application Support/caverec/gdrive_oauth_tokens.json on macOS, ~/.config/caverec/gdrive_oauth_tokens.json on Linux, with owner-only file permissions on macOS and Linux, or %APPDATA%\caverec\gdrive_oauth_tokens.json on Windows) or in your mobile device's secure credential storage, and communicate directly between your device and Google's API servers (googleapis.com) over HTTPS. We (the developer of Cave Recorder and Cave Companion) operate no intermediary servers and never receive, store, view, or transmit your Google account data, OAuth tokens, or audio and video files.

Cave Recorder's and Cave Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect or have access to any user data, no information is ever sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
