Cave Recorder (caverec)

A studio-grade multitrack digital audio workstation built for bands and engineers recording live in rehearsal caves and studios. Every song or jam gets its own independent 00:00:00.000 Take, simultaneous 32-bit float stems + live stereo Print, sub-sample multi-mic phase alignment, console mixing, and optional two-way Google Drive session & mixdown sync.

Rehearsal-Cave Insurance: Built for dimly lit basements where the drummer inevitably starts playing the best groove of the year five minutes before anyone remembers to press Record — which is why Cave Recorder keeps a continuous 30-minute rolling RAM ring ready to rescue the jam after the last cymbal fades.

Built Around Real Rehearsal & Tracking Rooms

Fixed dark-chassis console ergonomics — no floating plugin mazes or multi-hour timeline scrubbing.

RAM • RETRO (SHIFT + R) • 2-TRK PRINT CH 01–18

30-Minute Retroactive RAM Capture & Live Prints

A continuous lock-free 30-minute rolling RAM ring buffers all armed channels while the transport is stopped so you can capture a spontaneous jam after it happens. During every Take, Cave Recorder simultaneously writes 32-bit float pre-fader multitrack stems (rec/) and a live stereo Master Bus Print (mix/) ready for immediate band listening.

Hierarchy: Project → Session → Take (t=0) Chapter 3: Workflows →
GCC-PHAT • POLARITY (Ø) • SUB-SAMPLE DELAY ALIGN Φ

Sub-Sample Multi-Mic Phase Alignment (ALIGN Φ)

Acoustic arrival-time differences between close drum mics, overheads, room mics, and DI/cab pairs hollow out low-end punch. One click on ALIGN ALL runs GCC-PHAT cross-correlation against your chosen reference channel, automatically applying polarity inversion (Ø) and fractional all-pass time alignment stored in microseconds across sample rates.

Instant global A/B via PHASE: ALIGN / BYP Chapter 5: Phase & DSP →
12-BAND SVF EQ • VCA COMP • TAPE • CLAP 0-ALLOC AUDIO

Hardware-Inspired Mix Console & Built-In DSP

Every input strip and stereo Subgroup Bus features a reorderable 8-slot rack with a 12-band parametric EQ + 4096-point Dual-Trace RTA, 2D VCA compressor with transfer curve, analog tape saturation, zero-latency convolution reverb, capstan tape delay, true-peak master limiter, and native CLAP plugin hosting with automatic PDC.

Calibrated hardware loopback offset (G + R) Chapter 4: Mix Console →
PASS COMPING • PRESENT STASH • GOOGLE DRIVE SESSION REEL

Session Reel, Expandable Pass Comping & Present Stash

Every multitrack Take starts at its own 00:00:00.000 origin. Latching Overdub Mode (OD) records track-level Pass lanes (Rec001 Base Pass, Rec002-OD Overdub Pass) for non-destructive comping. Audition historical console Snapshots safely with Present Stash A/B recall, and optionally sync sessions or 24-bit _Mixes/ FLACs directly to your band's Google Drive.
