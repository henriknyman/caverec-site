## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added

- **Human Musicianship Manifesto & Curated Native Console Suite (`dev-w96`):** Complete studio mixing, dynamics, tone-shaping, and restoration suite engineered for live bands playing together in one room without beat-grid or pitch-correction bloat:
  - **Guitar & Bass `AmpRig` & Native `.nam` (`A2` / `A1`) Neural Amp Capture (`dev-w96.7` & `dev-w96.8`):** 8-model vacuum-tube amplifier processor (`[CIR]` mode with `InputCalibrator` and decoupled `CabinetMicProcessor`) paired with a zero-allocation real-time SIMD WaveNet `.nam` (`A2` / `A1` / Slimmable) neural amp inference engine (`[NAM]` mode), two-tier self-contained `<LibraryRoot>/.caverec/amp_captures/` + `<SessionFolder>/models/` / `<TakeFolder>/models/` storage, `ConsoleListView` Capture Browser, and a 3-step Guided Hardware Capture & Training modal (`CAP`) with real `audio_io` loopback/sweep SPSC capture, GCC-PHAT + clock-drift lock alignment, and background Accelerate AMX / SIMD SGEMM backpropagation training with live ESR convergence graphing and A/B auditioning.
  - **Zero-Slot `PhaseAlignNetwork` & Two-Mode Multicore Polarity & Phase Maximizer (`dev-w96.3`):** Top-of-strip 5-stage cascaded allpass phase rotator (`10 ms` clickless dual-branch crossfade) driven by **Mode 1 (Instant Binary Polarity Maximizer, `< 50 ms`)** across connected acoustic cliques (`Low-End Weight 30–200 Hz`, `Broadband Loudness ITU-R BS.1770-4 LUFS`, `Transient Crest & Impact 60 Hz–6 kHz`, and `Anti-Comb Spectral Smoothness`) and **Mode 2 (Deep Multicore Spectral Phase & Allpass Solver, `30 s .. 30 min`)** using a 256-bin Hermitian Cross-Spectral Density (`CSD`) tensor, Complex Torus SDP relaxation with live `Coherence Efficiency %` ceiling, Viterbi DP, Lang-Laakso WLS Riemannian polish, and Parallel Tempering with five Topological Tunneling Moves (`T1`, `T2a`, `T2b`, `T3`, `T4`) populating loudness-matched **Candidates A, B, C, D** with `[GAIN-MATCH AUDITION]` and `PolarityAuditionStash` protection.
  - **Context-Aware 3-Variant Noise Gate (`Expander`, `DrumBleed`, `AmpDynamic`, `dev-w96.2`):** Smooth downward `Expander`, differential `DrumBleed` tom/snare isolation gate with one-click `[LRN]` drum fundamental lock (`55–350 Hz`, `Q = 4.0`), `1.5 ms` lookahead, and envelope-driven Dynamic Decay Low-Pass Filter (`20 kHz -> 250–800 Hz`), plus the stoner/doom/psych `AmpDynamic` gate combining `8x`-decimated autocorrelation pitch periodicity (`H`) and Wiener spectral flatness (`SFM`) for `-34 dBFS` `Whisper Clean Lock`, `1.5–5.0 s` `Feedback Bloom Lock`, `15–40 ms` `C²` quintic S-curve `Palm-Mute Clamp`, and split-band `HF Tame` de-hissing.
  - **Multi-Topology `VCA` / `FET` / `Opto` Compressor (`dev-22n`):** Selectable feed-forward RMS (`VCA`), ultra-fast `0.02 ms` discrete transistor (`FET`), and program-dependent dual-release photocell (`Opto`) compressor topologies across all Input Tracks, `Subgroup` Buses, and the `Master Bus`, plus continuous Sidechain Spectral Tilt.
  - **12-Band Dynamic EQ, `VocalRider`, Split-Band `DeEsser` & `MicroPitchDoubler` (`dev-ed3` & `dev-w96.6`):** Per-band threshold-driven Dynamic EQ mode across all 12 parametric bands (`B1..B12`), zero-latency `VocalRider` (`±6 dB`) with speech-weighted detector and `Breath & Bleed Freeze Gate`, two-stage `DeEsser` with `120 Hz` Dynamic Low-Shelf Plosive Arrestor and `2.0–16.0 kHz` split-band sibilance suppressor (`SC LISTEN`), differential envelope `TransientShaper`, and mono-compatible quadrature `MicroPitchDoubler`.

### Changed

- **Unified Table-Driven 8-Slot Rack Architecture (`dev-w96.9`):** Refactored `RackSlotKind` and `BuiltinSlotKind` dispatch, conflict sanitization, and action translation across `engine_graph`, `session_store`, and `daw_ui` into a unified table-driven registry and decomposed large workspace modules into focused submodules.

### Fixed

- **Multi-Channel Audio Interface Selection & Input Routing (`dev-4es`):** Automatically prefer multi-channel studio audio interfaces over 2-channel built-in system microphones on startup while preserving a unified hardware output clock.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- **Core Recording Hierarchy (`Project` -> `Session` -> `Take` -> `Pass`):** Four-level studio organization with 08:00 local session day rollover, independent `00:00:00.000` (`t = 0`) multitrack `Take` timelines, expandable track-level `PassLane`s (`Rec001` Base Pass and `Rec002-OD` Overdub Passes) for non-destructive comping, `NEW TAKE` / `SPLIT TAKE` range extraction, and built-in session templates (`MicAndGuitar001` 2-channel and `GuitarBassDrumTrio001` 11-channel) plus custom `SAVE TEMPLATE` studio layouts up to 18 channels.
- **30-Minute Retroactive RAM Capture & Live Stereo `Print`:** Lock-free 30-minute rolling RAM ring buffer (`RETRO (SHIFT+R)`) across up to 18 input channels alongside simultaneous 32-bit IEEE float BWF `.wav` stem recording and live post-`Master Bus` 2-track `Print` capture.
- **Dark Heritage Console & Real-Time DSP Rack:** Six studio workspace views (`Mix Console`, `Waveform Timeline`, `Channel Inspector`, `Session Reel`, `Markers & Ranges`, and customizable 58-action `Keymap`) with a reorderable 8-slot per-strip and per-`Subgroup` processor rack (`12-Band Parametric EQ` with Dual-Trace RTA, `VCA Compressor`, `Tape Saturation`, and `Aux Send Tap`), dedicated stereo `AUX 1 REVERB` (partitioned FFT convolution reverb with dynamic `DCY` plate IR rebuild) and `AUX 2 TAPE DLY` returns, `Master Bus` tape/compressor/brickwall limiter with `2-TRK` live printing and `FLAC` & `MP3` `BOUNCE`, automatic Plugin Delay Compensation (`PDC`) with `Low-Latency Monitoring`, and hardware `Roundtrip Loopback Calibration` (`Measure Latency` and `RE-APPLY MEASURED OFFSET`).
- **Sub-Sample Multi-Mic Phase Alignment (`ALIGN Φ`):** GCC-PHAT cross-correlation phase optimizer computing polarity inversion (`Ø`) and microsecond-persisted sub-sample Thiran allpass fractional delay against a reference microphone.
- **24-Bit Lossless `FLAC` & 320 kbps CBR `MP3` `Print`s, `Mixdown`s, and `Stem Bounce`s:** One-click `FLAC PRINT` / `MP3 PRINT` conversion in the `Session Reel` and offline `Master Bus` `Mixdown` / per-track `Stem Bounce` exports with automatic `<ProjectTag>/_Mixes/` indexing for native web browser and mobile playback.
- **`Snapshot`s, `Present Stash` A/B Recall & Federated Google Drive Sync:** Immutable console `Snapshot`s with automatic `Present Stash` live state protection, plus native OAuth 2.0 PKCE Google Drive synchronization featuring `.caverec_project.json` CRDT `Project Cloud Index` discovery, `Linked Remote Project` sharing, `Causal Revision Lineage` conflict protection, and `.caverec_lock.json` `Session Lock` leases with `Read-Only Preview` mode.
- **Cross-Platform Desktop & Headless Distribution:** Native macOS (`aarch64` CoreAudio `.app` bundle), Windows (`x86_64` WASAPI `.exe`), and Linux (`x86_64` ALSA) builds paired with `caverec-headless` and the offline `Cave_Recorder_User_Manual.pdf`.
