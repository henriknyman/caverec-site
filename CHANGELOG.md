# Changelog

All notable changes to **Cave Recorder (`caverec`)** are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.4.5] - 2026-11-10

### Changed

- **DAW-Coupled Mobile Camera Recording Workflow (`dev-6v7`):** Replaced the standalone local-only Camera View record button on Android and iOS with a studio-synchronized **Camera Armed** toggle (`CAMERA ARMED FOR DAW RECORD`) plus **`RECORD DAW TAKE`** / **`STOP TAKE & SYNC`** remote transport controls so companion cameras start, finalize, and upload in lockstep with the desktop workstation's `Take` lifecycle.
- **Direct Clock-Synchronized Mobile Rear Torch Latency Calibration (`dev-sjr`):** Simplified on-phone 4-pulse Rear LED Torch calibration to solve camera sensor/ISP latency directly against UDP-clock-disciplined torch timestamps without requiring an on-phone speaker-to-microphone audio loop, and automatically combined the companion's optical offset with the desktop workstation's studio microphone calibration in the **Camera Calibration & Sync** modal.

### Fixed

- **Mobile Camera Recording Finalization & Streaming `.mp4` / `.vididx` Ingest (`dev-6v7`):** Fixed mobile camera recording finalization across Android (`Camera2` / `MediaCodec`) and iOS (`AVAssetWriter`) by adding streaming file-path finalization (`finish_camera_recording_from_path`, eliminating 64 MiB JNI byte-array limits), enforcing deterministic end-of-stream (`EOS`) encoder drain ordering, hardening teardown against transient clock-lock dips, and preventing re-entrant double-stop races.
- **Wireless LAN `:48049` Camera Angle Sync & Zero-Take Session Manifests (`dev-6v7`):** Fixed wireless LAN take video-angle transfer and desktop timeline ingestion so `0`-take sessions sync cleanly, `Camera + Player` role devices can push recorded video angles, remote takes are marked verified only after desktop manifest merge confirmation, and companion sync triggers immediately on desktop `Record` start and stop.

## [0.4.4] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- **Camera Angle Removal (`- REM CAM` / `DEL`), Multi-Lane Camera Filmstrips & Auto-Pruned Companion Cameras (`dev-yuw`):** Added `- REM CAM` and per-row `DEL` camera angle removal actions in the desktop camera mapping table, automatic synchronization and pruning of revoked or unassigned mobile companion camera entries from the paired device registry, and dedicated per-camera `wgpu` filmstrip lanes on the Waveform Timeline.
- **Enlarged Mobile Camera Viewfinder & Take Player Video Viewports (`dev-fdx`):** Enlarged the `16:9` mobile camera viewfinder and multi-angle Take Player video viewports across phone (`~220–340 dp`) and 2-pane tablet/landscape layouts, added sensor aspect-ratio scaling on Android portrait viewfinders, and moved the **Optical Flash Sync Calibration** controls into a collapsible drawer on iOS and Android.

### Fixed

- **End-to-End Mobile Companion 4-Pulse Rear Torch & Audio Camera Latency Calibration (`dev-eew`):** Fixed mobile companion 4-pulse camera latency calibration across Android and iOS by scheduling `80 ms` rear LED torch pulses anchored to `mapCameraFrameToSamplePos(nowNs)`, emitting the `3.0 s` 4-pulse Vernier audio click stimulus, capturing live microphone audio and camera Y-plane luminance ramps, and computing sub-frame calibrated camera latency (`device_calib_micros`).
- **Android Companion `STOP & PUSH TO DAW` Camera Recording Finalization & LAN Sync Retry (`dev-yuw`):** Synchronized Android `Camera2` / `MediaCodec` recording finalization before triggering wireless LAN upload and added automatic LAN sync retry when pushing recorded `.mp4` / `.vididx` camera angles to the desktop workstation.
- **Revoked Mobile Companion Re-Enrollment with Rotated Pairing Credentials (`dev-u40`):** Fixed paired device registry credential watermarks so a previously revoked mobile companion (`device_id`) can cleanly re-enroll when scanning a newly rotated desktop pairing QR code or 6-digit PIN.
